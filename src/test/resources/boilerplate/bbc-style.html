Rail operator fined over cancelled night services

By Priya Nandakumar BBN transport reporter 20 August 2026

The regulator has fined Northline Rail £4.2m for cancelling scheduled night services on 41 occasions without giving passengers the required notice.

Its report found that the operator had removed the trains from its live timetable feed less than an hour before departure, so that stations displayed them as running until shortly before midnight.

What the regulator found

Investigators described the practice as systematic rather than accidental, pointing to an internal instruction that told controllers to delay publication of cancellations until crew rosters were confirmed.

Northline said it accepted the findings in full and had changed the instruction in June.

The fine will be paid into the passenger compensation fund.
